On publishing the safety prompt. We publish the full production safety prompt on purpose. Good safety never relies on the rules being secret — the output gate (which inspects every reply before a child sees it, and fails safe) is the guarantee, not the prompt. Showing the actual rules is how a parent earns the right to trust them.
