This update is about the way Valence arrives on a PC: the installer now teaches.

![The installer's new heads-up page - it quotes the question Windows is about to ask and explains why]({{ '/assets/img/installer-headsup.png' | relative_url }})

## What's new

- **The installer walks you through setup.** A fresh install gets a heads-up before Windows asks its "unknown publisher" question - the exact words it will use, the honest reason it appears (Valence is new to Windows' seen-it-many-times list; it does not mean something is wrong), and exactly what Yes and No each do.
- **First-launch expectations, up front.** The final page tells you what to expect the first time you open Valence: the one-time Windows Defender look, the AI model download, and the firewall question about llama-server (your built-in AI engine) with the exact button to click - plus a Getting Started link and an "Open Valence now" checkbox.
- **Updates stay quick.** Upgrading? The wizard skips the walkthrough and finishes with a single line. You are only ever taught once, on a fresh install.
- **Valence's own mark** on every wizard page, replacing the stock installer clip-art.
