A small fix: the buy links inside the app now take you straight to checkout.

## Fixes

- **"Get a license" opens checkout.** Settings ▸ License ▸ **Get a license** and the **Purchase a license** link shown when your trial ends now open the Android checkout page directly, instead of the website's pricing page.

Everything in 1.5.21 is unchanged.

## Known issues

- **A rewrite can drop the last bullet of a section.** The "Check these" list flags what is missing, so check it before you use the result.
- **A brief dark corner** can flash when you turn some phones sideways.
- **Scrolling** can be a little less smooth than it should be while the background is showing.
