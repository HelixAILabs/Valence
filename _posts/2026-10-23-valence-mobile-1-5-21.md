Long documents no longer stop at "too large". Valence now offers to rewrite them in sections, keeps going with the screen off, and gets itself unstuck if the on-device model hangs. Appearance settings now live in one place.

## New

- **Rewrite a long document in sections.** Paste a document that is too long for one reply and Valence offers to rewrite it in sections instead of showing an error. Tap **Rewrite it in sections**, **No thanks**, or **Details**.
- **Summary, full rewrite and a "Check these" list.** The finished message starts with a short summary, then the whole rewrite, then a list of facts worth checking against your original.
- **Keeps working with the screen off.** A long rewrite carries on while your phone is locked, and a notification tells you when it is ready. You can pause, continue or resume it, and chat goes first while it runs.
- **Recovers by itself.** If the on-device model stops responding, Valence restarts it and answers the message that was waiting. If that fails, it tells you in the chat.
- **PDF text keeps its line breaks.** Text read from a PDF no longer runs a whole page together.

## Changes and fixes

- **One Appearance sheet.** Light or dark, accent colour, glow and motion, and the background are all in the menu's Appearance sheet. Settings ▸ Appearance opens the same sheet.
- **No false "Valence closed unexpectedly".** Stopping an answer that was using a tool no longer shows a crash banner the next time you open the app.

## Known issues

- **A rewrite can drop the last bullet of a section.** The "Check these" list flags what is missing, so check it before you use the result.
- **A brief dark corner** can flash when you turn some phones sideways.
- **Scrolling** can be a little less smooth than it should be while the background is showing.
