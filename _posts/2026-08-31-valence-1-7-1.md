Kids Mode's alerts had a timing problem: they fired while the child was still using their profile, and several buttons on the parent dashboard didn't do anything yet. This release fixes the whole path &mdash; from noticing something, to telling you about it, to letting you act on it.

## What's new

- **Notifications wait until you're back.** Alerts used to appear while your child was mid-conversation &mdash; on their screen, about them. Now they hold quietly and arrive when you switch to a grown-up profile, so they reach you instead of the person they're about.
- **Every alert opens what it's about.** Clicking a notification takes you straight to the conversation that triggered it, and a badge on the bell tells you how many are waiting.
- **The bell disappears in Kids Mode.** Notification history is a record of concerns about your child. It is now completely hidden while their profile is active &mdash; not just dimmed.
- **Better at noticing indirect language.** Children rarely say difficult things plainly. Valence now recognises phrasings it used to miss &mdash; the same sentence with an extra word dropped in the middle, or a feeling described sideways instead of directly.
- **And quieter where it was noisy.** One class of false alarm is gone: ordinary sentences that happened to share their shape with concerning ones &mdash; "nobody would notice if I wore a different shirt" &mdash; no longer raise an alert.
- **Crisis moments are handled from what your child says.** Valence now reads the child's own message for signs of crisis rather than only checking its own reply, so support surfaces even when the conversation takes an unexpected turn.

## Fixes

- Conversation tips in the parent dashboard open real guidance instead of doing nothing.
- Concerning messages are highlighted in the transcript with a short explanation of why they were flagged.
- Marking a conversation reviewed records who reviewed it and when.
- The quiet hours toggle applies.

## Note on first launch

Because the detection changed, Valence re-checks previously saved conversations once after updating. You may see flags on older chats that weren't there before &mdash; that's the improved detection looking back, not something new happening.

Detection helps you notice things worth a conversation. It isn't a guarantee, and it isn't a substitute for knowing your kid.
