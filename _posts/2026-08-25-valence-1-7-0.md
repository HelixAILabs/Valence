The biggest visual update Valence has ever shipped. Same workspace, same everything-where-you-left-it: dressed the way it always should have been.

![The premium reskin, glass surfaces, one accent color across the whole app, and the new appearance picker in the top bar]({{ '/assets/img/premium-reskin.png' | relative_url }})

## What's new

- **Glass, everywhere it counts.** The composer, menus, panels and windows sit on layered glass surfaces with depth and light behind them. The send button became something you actually want to press.
- **One color, the whole app follows.** Pick an accent color and every button, ring, highlight and chart retints to match: one choice, applied everywhere.
- **Change the look from the top bar.** The new appearance picker puts themes, your accent color and visual effects one click away instead of a trip into Settings, with an effects toggle that tones the glass down without changing anything else.
- **A typeface of its own.** Valence now ships with Barlow, bundled inside the app: identical on every machine, never phones home for a font.
- **A calmer top bar.** Token usage is a small donut, the notification bell and autosave tick appear only when they have something to say, and long model names shorten only when space actually runs out.
- **X-Ray grew up.** The response inspector is reorganized into four views, Story, Context, Model and Engine, behind one row of chips. The new Engine view charts your model's performance over time: reading speed, writing speed and cache reuse, turn after turn.

## Fixes

- The font picker works again, and menus size themselves to their contents.
- The update banner is legible in every theme.
- Very narrow windows keep every control reachable instead of clipping them.
- Switching local models mid-answer waits for the current reply to stop cleanly; panels no longer close themselves mid-use.
- Pressing Enter in the composer arms the full send sequence reliably.
- A Kids Mode gap closed: the new engine-performance route is gated by the parental X-Ray toggle like every other X-Ray surface.
