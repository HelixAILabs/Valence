This release is mostly about one thing: the app getting out of your way. Answers arrive smoothly, they finish, and when your AI remembers something it shows you what it used.

We previewed this work here: [the pause fix]({{ '/2026/08/24/valence-1-6-preview.html' | relative_url }}) and [the memory redesign]({{ '/2026/08/13/memory-architecture-preview.html' | relative_url }}).

![The new Performance card in X-Ray. Read and write time, tokens per second, and cache reuse, measured by the engine itself]({{ '/assets/img/xray-performance.png' | relative_url }})

## What's new

- **The pause is gone.** Replies used to stall partway and land in a lump. A stuck internal check could hold back the entire rest of an answer. Fixed, along with the layers under it: streaming no longer waits on disk writes, no longer re-measures the window sixty times a second, and no longer slows down as answers grow.
- **Answers that actually finish.** The reply budget now comes from your model's real capacity instead of a fixed number, about double the old ceiling on a typical setup. When a limit genuinely is reached, Valence says so instead of trailing off mid-sentence.
- **Local models run faster.** GPU use is now sized to your hardware instead of forced, and Qwen 3.8 loads at last (updated bundled engine).
- **Memory that answers the question.** &ldquo;Did we talk about X?&rdquo; actually searches; delivered memories get used; paraphrases land; junk stops crowding out real notes; stranded memories from other profiles come back automatically. One &ldquo;memory recalled&rdquo; chip sits with the answer it informed.
- **X-Ray shows the receipts.** A new Performance card with the engine's own measurements, and X-Ray no longer asserts things it cannot know.
- **Kids Mode, wider crisis detection**: indirect phrasing is recognised, the AI's own replies are checked, and a model must pass our red-team to be offered at all. Re-audited for this release: 1,000 fresh adversarial turns per model against the exact shipping safety system, zero harmful replies reached the child. [Read the full audit](https://helixailabs.com/Valence%20-%20Kids%20Safety%20Proof.html). **Correction, 6 September 2026:** that "zero harmful replies" figure is withdrawn — the grader that produced it was later tested and found to be marking every reply safe without reading it. [What we found and what changed]({{ '/2026/09/06/we-tested-our-own-safety-test.html' | relative_url }}).

## Fixes

- Hover buttons on messages are clickable again (including Kids read-aloud); hidden menu items no longer steal clicks; the tall gap under replies is gone.
- Local maths renders properly typeset; Repeat Penalty can no longer be set to a value that produces gibberish.
- X-Ray content stays inside its window; the context meter agrees with the model's real window.
- Valence's own icon finally appears in Alt-Tab and taskbar previews.
