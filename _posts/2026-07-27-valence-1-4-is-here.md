## Privacy you can see

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Private, even on the device.</b> Conversations can now be encrypted at rest &mdash; locked on disk, so no one else who uses the computer can read them.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>One switch for &ldquo;stays on this machine.&rdquo;</b> A new Local-Only mode blocks every optional network call. It won't block the things you explicitly ask for &mdash; downloading a model, or importing your old chats &mdash; and we say so plainly rather than over-claiming.</div>

## A space built for kids

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Locked down by default.</b> Kids Mode now blocks everything a child shouldn't reach unless a parent opens it. Protection is the default, not something you remember to switch on.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Real help in a crisis.</b> If a child in distress mentions self-harm, Valence reliably surfaces the <b>988 Suicide &amp; Crisis Lifeline</b> rather than trying to handle it alone.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>A parent console.</b> Review your child's activity and emotional patterns at a glance, with the moments that matter surfaced first &mdash; and nothing leaving your PC.</div>

## A calmer, more honest everyday AI

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Replies that arrive, not flicker.</b> While your AI works you see a quiet &ldquo;responding&rdquo; moment, then the finished reply appears once, cleanly formatted. Want to watch it stream? One click.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Your AI, by name.</b> Models show a friendly name &mdash; like &ldquo;Gemma 4 &mdash; Balanced&rdquo; &mdash; instead of a cryptic ID, in the picker and on every reply.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Honest about your hardware.</b> First-run setup no longer claims a graphics card you don't have, and on a slower machine it says so plainly while your first model warms up.</div>

<div class="v14s-feat"><b>Context that fits your PC (1.4.1).</b> An on-device model used to keep only a short, fixed slice of the conversation in view. Valence now sizes that window to your actual hardware &mdash; following much longer conversations where your machine allows, staying lean where it doesn't.</div>
