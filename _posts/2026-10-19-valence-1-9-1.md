Kids chats stay protected after updating.

## Fixes

- **Older Kids chats are protected too.** If a parent continued a child's chat in an earlier version, Valence now recognises it as a Kids chat the first time 1.9.1 starts. Pictures stay unloaded, it isn't sent to HIVE, and Valence asks before sending it to a cloud model.
- **A small fix.** The setup screen no longer calls every installer "slim".
