Two fixes, one of them for Kids Mode.

- **Kids profiles can be created right after setup.** After installing the model, Kids Mode asked you to run a "readiness & benchmark probe" in Settings before it would create a kids profile, even though setup had just loaded the model and said it was ready. Setup's own check now counts.
- **The speed under the message box belongs to the current profile.** After switching from a kids profile to an adult one, the speed of the child's last reply stayed on screen. It now appears again only after the next reply.
