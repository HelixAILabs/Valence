Thinking now shows how long it took.

![Valence step trail - a local model thinks for a second, runs the calculator, analyzes the result, then answers 77,076]({{ '/assets/img/valence-1-8-step-trail.png' | relative_url }})

## What's new

- **Thinking shows time, not characters.** The step trail above a reply now reads "Thought for 6s" instead of a character count, so you can see at a glance how long the model spent thinking before it answered.

## Fixes

- After a tool runs &mdash; the calculator, the clock or a web search &mdash; the step reads "Analyzed the result". It used to say "search results" even when no search happened.

1.8.1 also carries everything in [1.8.0]({{ '/2026/10/05/valence-1-8-0.html' | relative_url }}): the step trail, the four-control composer, web search with your own free Tavily key, the Kids Mode safety fixes, and the Qwen 3.8 27B Balanced download fix.
