Clearer first setup, and fewer false "chat nearly full" warnings.

- **You can see what happens after the model downloads.** The first time the model loads, Valence tries each chip your phone has (AI chip, graphics chip, main processor) and keeps the fastest. That takes about half a minute and used to show only a counter. Now a panel shows which chip is being tried, what each one managed, and why it only happens once.
- **"Chat nearly full" no longer appears after a few short messages.** A web search's results were being counted as if they stayed in the chat. They don't, and the warning now only appears when the chat really is filling up.
