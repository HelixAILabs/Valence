Choose how much of the conversation the model can see, right from the model menu.

- **Conversation size, in the model menu.** Open the model menu (tap the model name at the top) and use the **Conversation size** slider to choose how much of the chat the model can see at once. The range is what your phone can handle. Each position says what it means: **Default**, **Larger** (uses more memory, still within what your phone is rated for), or, on phones with 8 GB of memory or more, **Above this phone's rating** (shown in a warning colour, and it may load slowly or fall back to a smaller size).
- **It applies right away.** Tap **Apply** and the model reloads at the new size there and then, so your next message doesn't wait for it. **Use the default** puts it back.
- The slider is locked while a reply is being written, and kids profiles always use the default size.
