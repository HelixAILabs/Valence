You can now see the model loading, and there is no more empty reply bubble.

![Valence loading a local model - one status line above the reply shows the model name, 4 seconds elapsed and its load steps]({{ '/assets/img/valence-1-8-2-loading-row.png' | relative_url }})

## What's new

- **You can see the model loading.** The first message after starting Valence shows one status line above the reply &mdash; the model's name, its load steps and how many seconds it has taken &mdash; instead of an empty reply bubble.
- **The answer shows as it is written.** The reply bubble appears only when the answer starts, and the text shows from the first word. Before that, the status line says what is happening: "Reading your message...", or the name of the file it is reading.

## Fixes

- While the model loads, the animated background now runs behind the top bar and the message box too, instead of stopping at solid strips.
- "Loading weights" and "Responding" no longer sit side by side inside an empty bubble.

1.8.2 also carries everything in [1.8.1]({{ '/2026/10/05/valence-1-8-1.html' | relative_url }}) and [1.8.0]({{ '/2026/10/05/valence-1-8-0.html' | relative_url }}).
