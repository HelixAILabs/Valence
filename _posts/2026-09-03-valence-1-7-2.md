A much smaller download, and a shortcut icon that stays put.

## What's new

- **The installer is dramatically smaller.** Slim drops from 310&nbsp;MB to 122&nbsp;MB, and the all-in-one from 808&nbsp;MB to 620&nbsp;MB. A packaging mistake meant every installer we have shipped carried a second, redundant copy of the whole application inside it &mdash; used for nothing but the shortcut picture. It is gone.
- **It also stops eating your disk.** That redundant copy was written into a hidden Windows folder on every install and never cleaned up &mdash; roughly 180&nbsp;MB per install, left behind for good. New installs write a 228&nbsp;KB icon instead, and upgrading reclaims the copy your current version left behind.
- **The shortcut keeps its icon.** The Valence icon on your Start Menu and desktop shortcuts could go blank after installing an update. It now uses a proper multi-resolution icon file, so it stays sharp everywhere it appears.
