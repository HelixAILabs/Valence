Four rules we won't break.

Helix is independent, with one bias: keep you in control of your own AI. Every product we make is shaped around the same four promises.

i.

Privacy

Local-first by default. What happens on your machine stays on your machine: nothing phones home.

ii.

Ownership

Buy the tool, own the tool. No subscriptions, no rug-pulls, no renting your own software.

iii.

Observability

See what the AI can reach and what it does. No hidden actions, no quiet background calls.

iv.

Transparency

Your data is plain files you can open and read. Our posture is stated plainly, not buried.

Different tools. Same promise.

From a cross-model AI app to private data tools, agent automation, and learning for kids. Every Helix product is built local-first and yours to keep. One is shipping today; the rest are in the lab.
